# Migrating old databases

Currently mostly busy with modernizing 15 years old infrastructure of some
customer.
Old and huge MySQL databses. No foreign keys. No timestamps. It's all pretty
convoluted.
Many VM's running directly on KVM without libvirt abstraction. Failing hard
drives. Old PHP. And the overaly of many "big brain" sysadmins.

But at least, I gained full access to the most of the resources, restored
passwords, established secure remote access, etc.

Now it's all about writing migration scripts. Convert databases from MyISAM to
InnoDB, clean up security issues, etc.

Ultimate goal is to write automation for fully containerized and clean environment.

While all this seems frustrating to "dig into old garbage" in some sense I like
it. It's like hunting. :smiley:

Learned about Flyway SQL migrations. Established IPMI and HP iLO access.
